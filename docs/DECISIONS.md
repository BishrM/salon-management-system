# SalonOps — Decision log

Source of truth, in priority order: explicit business requirements (`SRS.md`, owner instructions) →
approved Rev 6 plan (accounting and technical rules) → existing implementation where consistent.
Business decisions D1–D4 and B1–B15 are listed at the end; B1–B15 are implemented with the proposed
defaults and **still need the owner's confirmation**.

| # | Decision | Reason | Alternatives considered | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | One `entries` table with a `kind` column (service/product/expense/zakat/food) and per-kind CHECKs, instead of five tables. Read-only views keep the Rev 6 / SRS names (`service_sales`, `product_sales`, `expenses`, `zakat_entries`, `food_deductions`). | Rev 6 §3.2 gives all five the same common columns, evidence rules, correction/void/late/sync lifecycle. One table = one correction path, one sync path, one late-entry path. | Five tables with duplicated RPCs and triggers. | Schema detail only; no business rule changes. |
| D-02 | `product_catalog` created in the M1-completion migration. | Rev 6 §11 lists it in M1; the pre-existing migration had deferred it ("M1 authorisation excludes products"), which conflicts with the plan and the business scope. | Leave for later. | Products can be sold from day one. |
| D-03 | Staff may have one *home* and one *temporary* assignment overlapping (exclusion constraint on staff, is_temporary, date range). Temporary assignments always have an end date. | Business requirement "temporary cross-outlet assignments". The existing constraint forbade any overlap, forcing a loaned employee's home assignment to be ended. | End and re-create home assignment (loses clarity). | Loans are recorded without touching history. |
| D-04 | **All** register writes (online and offline) go through `sync_ops`; `public.sync_ops` (op log) is the idempotency store. | One code path; online and offline behave identically; retries and duplicates are handled the same way. | Separate online RPC (`record_entry`) + offline sync. | Rev 6 §4.1's `record_entry`/`correct_entry`/`void_entry` are op types of `sync_ops`. |
| D-05 | Kept the existing permission matrix (`app.has_permission`) as the role check. | It implements Rev 6's `app.has_role` validity predicate and the §4 matrix exactly, and is already tested. Added `catalog.manage`, `reports.read`, `outlet.details`. | Rewrite as `has_role`. | No change to who can do what. |
| D-06 | Undecided late entries block *submission* (blocker `late_entries_undecided`) as well as approval. Late **food payments** follow the same include/exclude review. | Rev 6 §5.6 requires an explicit decision per late entry; a revision must not silently absorb an undecided one. Food payments are offline-capable and can also arrive for a frozen day. | Include undecided entries automatically. | Management decides every late row; nothing is silently included or dropped. |
| D-07 | All entered amounts are **whole rupees** (stored as integer paise, CHECK `amount % 100 = 0`). | Salon prices, Paytm counter amounts and cash denominations are whole rupees; it makes the per-employee exact 10 % share an exact integer (J3) with no rounding inside the statement. | Allow paise (exact share would need fractional paise). | A price such as ₹99.50 cannot be entered. Reversible: relax the CHECKs and store `exact_share` as numeric. |
| D-08 | Plain Tailwind components instead of shadcn/ui. | A dozen small shared components cover the UI; avoids generating and maintaining a component library. | shadcn/ui (SRS §12 suggestion). | Visual only. |
| D-09 | Excel export with a small built-in XLSX writer (`lib/xlsx.ts`) instead of exceljs. PDF via print stylesheet (O11). | exceljs 4.4.0 depends on a `uuid` version with an open advisory; the only "fix" is a downgrade, which is not allowed. The writer is ~120 lines using Node's `zlib.crc32`. | exceljs; CSV only. | Zero production dependency vulnerabilities. |
| D-10 | **Paytm: manual daily total + optional CSV statement import with explicit column mapping.** No API integration. | Official Paytm docs show no transaction-list API for static QR/Soundbox merchants: Transaction Status needs our own order IDs and Settlement APIs return settled T+1 data (sources below). The statement export layout is not verified, so the importer maps columns instead of assuming one. | Building against an unverified API (forbidden: "never invent API functionality"). | Closing is fully usable; imported transactions are de-duplicated by reference and compared with the manual total (warning `paytm_import_mismatch`). |
| D-11 | Hand-written service worker (`public/sw.js`) and raw IndexedDB outbox instead of Serwist + idb. | Next 16 builds with Turbopack; a 50-line worker covers the needed caching (static cache-first, pages network-first, API never cached). | Serwist, Workbox, idb. | Two fewer dependencies. |
| D-12 | Approval requires explicit acknowledgement of: a non-zero variance, the two "service Paytm" flags, and undated offline entries that may belong to the day. | Rev 6 §5.3/§5.4 require acknowledgement of variance and Paytm flags; §6.3 says the closing shows a warning while unassigned entries exist — acknowledgement makes the approver see it. | Warning only. | One extra checkbox in rare cases. |
| D-13 | "Previous day" for the opening float = latest business date on/after the initial-float date that has a closing **or** active entries. Days with entries but no closing block approval of later days. | Days with no trading need no closing; days with trading must not be skipped in the cash chain. | Require a closing for every calendar day. | Closed days need no paperwork. |
| D-14 | A food-payment reversal is dated by its own receipt time and affects that day's drawer. | The reversal represents cash coming back at that moment. | Back-date to the original payment (would touch a frozen day). | No frozen day is ever changed. |
| D-15 | A device clock running backwards relative to an earlier op of the same device (beyond the online window) is flagged `seq_order` and goes to Unassigned. | Rev 6 §6.3 lists "device_seq out of order" as a suspect condition. | Ignore. | Rare; management dates such entries. |
| D-16 | Commit-time (deferred) constraint triggers run `SECURITY DEFINER`. | Found by the two-session test: at COMMIT they run outside the RPC, as the client role, and could not read their helpers — every real handover would have failed. | — | Bug fixed before release. |
| D-17 | Users are created by the owner with a temporary password (service-role key used only in that server action, after a `users.manage` check through the owner's own session). Public sign-up is disabled. | No email (SMTP) is configured; owner-invited accounts per Rev 6 §4. | Email invites (needs SMTP, an external account setting). | Owner hands the password over in person. |
| D-18 | Dashboard visible to owner, manager and head; register to all roles. | SRS §5.3 head "viewing outlet sales"; §5.5 counter "viewing relevant daily totals" (shown on the Register). | Owner/manager only. | — |
| D-19 | Local database tests run on plain PostgreSQL 17 with a stub of Supabase's `auth` schema and a pgTAP-compatible shim (`supabase/tests/local/`). CI also runs the genuine `supabase test db`. | Docker could not start on the development machine (disk 99 % full) and building pgTAP from source was not permitted in this session. | Wait for Docker. | Same test files run in both; the shim implements only the pgTAP functions used. |
| D-20 | Realtime publication on `entries` and `closing_events`; dashboard also polls every 60 s. | Live dashboard (SRS §7.1) with a fallback if Realtime is unavailable. | Polling only. | — |
| D-21 | Audit rows without an outlet (global `staff` and `profiles` tables) are visible only to a holder of `audit.read` in an outlet the staff member is assigned to / the profile is a member of; other outlet-less rows are hidden (migration `…001200`). | Staging S12 (2026-11-19) showed any owner could read every outlet's staff and user names through these rows — a cross-outlet leak (defect D3). | Add `outlet_id` to staff/profile audit rows (staff and profiles can belong to several outlets). | Security fix; owners still see their own staff and members' history; no business rule changes. |
| D-22 | Removing paid food is one op: `void_entry` with `reverse_payments: true` reverses every unreversed payment and voids the food in one subtransaction (migration `…001300`). Reversal ids derive from the op id. A plain void of paid food is refused with an actionable message. | Staging: the Register sent a plain void, the database refused it, and two client-side ops (reverse, then void) could leave a reversal without the void. | Two ops; auto-reverse on every void (would move cash without the user seeing it). | No accounting rule changes: reversal dated by receipt time as before (D-14). |
| D-23 | The sender of a **failed** op may withdraw it with a reason (`withdraw_sync_op`); it stays stored as failed, resolved by the sender, in the audit log. Conflicts stay management-only. Blocked results name the op they wait for. | Ops behind a failure were blocked until a manager resolved it; the device that made it had no way to, and "Clear from this device" hid the cause. | Auto-unblock on failure (could apply out of intended order); deleting failed ops. | Nothing is deleted, skipped or marked applied; waiting ops replay through normal validation. |
| D-24 | Sign-out with unsent ops: synced → sign out; pending → warning; failed/blocked → type KEEP; allowed only if `navigator.storage.persisted()` (else blocked, with the reason). Queue rows stay scoped to user + outlet; the outbox API itself filters by user (list, export, clear). | The guard trapped users in an account during demos; "Save pending entries" exported every user's rows. | Disable the guard; clear the queue on sign-out (loses sales). | Account switching without data loss; another login never sees, sends, exports or clears someone else's ops. |
| D-25 | A **blocked** op (never stored) can be withdrawn by the session that holds it: `withdraw_blocked_op` stores it as failed under its own op id, device and seq, then withdraws it through `withdraw_sync_op` (resolved by the sender, reason, audited). Only an op that is blocked right now; anything the server already has is refused. Migration `…001400`. | Staging 2026-11-20 (defect D7): changes to entries the server does not have were blocked forever; nothing on the server could be withdrawn or resolved, so the only way out was wiping browser storage. | Fail ops on missing entries (would break out-of-order arrival); client-only discard (no record, could be re-sent). | Withdrawn ops are duplicates on any re-send and never execute. The server cannot know who wrote an op it never stored, so whoever withdraws it is recorded as its submitter (same as `sync_ops`). |
| D-26 | Every **service** sale records how it was paid, reusing `payment_method` (`cash` = Cash, `paytm` = UPI / Paytm / Card). Required by the RPC on every new service sale and service correction; service rows from before 2026-11-20 keep NULL ("not recorded"). The closing formulas are unchanged (physical cash still uses the Paytm total entered at closing); `day_summary` adds the recorded split and warns `paytm_recorded_mismatch` when recorded UPI/Paytm/Card (services + products) ≠ the entered Paytm total, only when no service row of the day lacks a method. Migration `…001500`. | Client feature 4 (2026-11-20): Cash and UPI / Paytm / Card buttons under the service calculator. | A separate UPI/card channel (rejected: UPI and card settle through Paytm, Rev 6 §5.4); deriving physical cash from the recorded split (rejected: the Paytm statement stays authoritative). | Commission is still 10% of all service sales rounded down to ₹10, whatever the method. Ops queued before the release without a method fail with a clear message and can be withdrawn. |
| D-27 | **EDITED** = corrected at least once, derived as `version − 1 − (voided ? 1 : 0) > 0` (only corrections and voids raise `version`, a void at most once and last). No new column. Shown in the Register and the Owner dashboard; for owners the tag links to the record's audit history (original → corrected values, editor, server time, device time of the op, reason). Corrections stay in-place updates audited by `entries_audit` (immutable `audit_logs`, owner-only, per outlet). Products can now be corrected from the Register (quantity, payment method). | Client feature 5 (2026-11-20). | A stored `edited` flag (rejected: the guard trigger would need it in the mutable list and it can be derived exactly). | Frozen days still refuse corrections (SO423) until management reopens the closing. |

| D-28 | **Two account types** (client, 2026-11-23). Owner side: `owner`, plus `manager` kept unchanged as the owner's son's management login (owner confirmed 2026-11-23). Operations: **one shared Shop/Staff login per outlet** (role `shop`, migrations `…001600`/`…001700`), used by every employee, the counter and the owner when present. Shop/Staff = the former head permissions + `staff.manage`; no approval, reopen, float, review, reports, users, audit or catalogue rights. `head`/`counter` stay in the enum and in stored memberships (history) but **grant nothing**; acting-head delegation is switched off (`delegation.manage` is held by nobody). One active Shop/Staff membership per outlet and one outlet per Shop/Staff login (unique indexes + `add_membership`); `add_membership` refuses `head`/`counter`. **Supersedes** D-05 (role list only; the matrix mechanism is unchanged), D-18 (head → Shop/Staff), B5 (acting head retired), A1 (Shop/Staff does the closing). | Client revision: no individual employee logins, no Head/Acting Head/Counter accounts or handover between them. | Delete legacy memberships (rejected: history and audit refer to them); map head/counter to Shop/Staff (rejected: silently widens old counter logins). | Existing head/counter logins lose access when the migration is applied; the owner must create the outlet's Shop/Staff login. The audit actor of operational work is now the shared login, which is stated honestly in the UI. |
| D-29 | **Employees are managed by Shop/Staff and deactivated, never deleted.** Deactivation sets `staff.inactive_from` = the next **business** date by trigger (outlet timezone + cutoff of the employee's current assignment; amended 2026-11-23 by migration `…002000`, before that the India calendar date + 1); reactivation clears it. `app.staff_assigned` is now date-aware: valid if assigned on the date and (active or date < `inactive_from`). Employees deactivated before 2026-11-23 keep `inactive_from` NULL and stay invalid on every date, exactly as before. The Employees screen no longer links logins. | Deactivated employees must stay valid for their past days (corrections, late entries, closing attribution) and disappear from current lists. Previously `active=false` invalidated them on **every** date. | Ending all assignments on deactivation (rejected: assignment history would be rewritten); backfilling old inactive rows from the audit log (rejected: not needed, riskier). | A deactivated employee can still be chosen for dates before deactivation; deletion stays impossible (trigger). `staff` is global: a Shop/Staff login may rename/deactivate an employee who also works at another outlet (U2). |
| D-30 | **"Who performed the closing?"**: `closing_drafts.closed_by_staff_id` set with `set_closing_employee` (Shop/Staff or management, editable states only), copied into each immutable revision. Required to submit (blockers `closing_employee_missing`, `closing_employee_invalid`); validated against assignment + activity **on the business date** (`closing_employees` lists exactly those). Revisions before 2026-11-23 keep NULL (`rev_closed_by` is `NOT VALID`). Attribution only: `submitted_by`, `closing_events.actor` and `audit_logs.actor` remain the authenticated login. A change after reopening is audited on `closing_drafts` (old → new) and the earlier revision keeps the original. | Client requirement; the shared login cannot say which person closed. | Storing it on `closing_events` (rejected: revisions must be self-contained snapshots). | Shown on the closing, approvals list, statement and reports (and export). |
| D-31 | **Revised food rule from `outlets.food_rule_v2_from`** (set to the day migration `…001800` is applied; earlier days keep D2/B6 exactly): breakfast is a salon expense (`food_shop_funded` = breakfast, also in `food_breakfast_salon`); lunch + dinner are deducted from the commission pool up to C (`food_commission_funded`); any rest is **owed to the salon by the employees** (`food_employee_owed`), at outlet level, with no approval step. Final balance = C − (lunch + dinner). Commission pool, rounding, payable C − FC, expected drawer and SRS net cash are unchanged; the bridge identity subtracts the owed amount so it still holds exactly. Repayments are recorded separately (`food_repayments`, owner/manager only, cash or UPI/Paytm/Card, date, employees named for information, note) and are received **outside the drawer**: they never enter a closing or drawer figure. Outstanding = owed on approved closings − net repayments; insert-only, corrected by a reversal with a reason. **Supersedes** SRS §3.3 "breakfast and lunch from commission", D2's excess handling and B6 for days on/after the start date (owner confirmed all three on 2026-11-23). | Client revision 2026-11-23. | Deduct breakfast too (old rule); shop absorbs the excess (B6); repayments into the drawer (would change ED and the revision formulas). | A day's debt counts only after approval; a reopened day drops out until re-approved (outstanding can show "repaid more than currently owed" meanwhile). No per-employee debt is created (U1). |
| D-32 | **Split payments, tips and the Daily Sheet** (migrations `…002100`, `…002200`, 2026-11-24). A service sale may be paid `split`: one entry, one employee, one `amount`, with `split_cash + split_paytm = amount` (both ≥ 0, checked by `app.entry_values` and a table constraint). A tip is entered explicitly on the sale (`tip_cash`, `tip_paytm`); it is never part of `amount`, so service sales, commission and allocations are unchanged. The **Paytm statement total entered at closing stays the authority** for cash sales (`S + P − paytm_total`); `day_summary` compares it with the Register's UPI / Paytm / Card (services incl. split parts + products + digital tips) as `paytm_difference` and the existing `paytm_recorded_mismatch` warning. The Daily Sheet (`/closing/sheet`) is read-only over the Register and `day_summary`; opening it records only `sheet_opens` (no closing, revision, event or notification). The owner's **Sheet changes** page (`sheet_changes`, `audit.read`) lists every Register addition, correction and removal to a day made after its sheet was first opened, one item per `audit_logs` row, with before/after section totals rebuilt from the audit log. | Client request 2026-11-24 (two PTM columns are a paper convenience; customers pay part cash, part UPI; tips). | A separate `tip` entry kind (would need every total to exclude it); computing Paytm from the Register (the statement is the authority); a notification table (would duplicate the audit log). | Tip cash handling is U5; notification scope and channel are U6. |
| D-32 | Commission handover recipient: **any employee assigned to and active at the outlet on the business date** (was: an employee linked to a valid head login). Amounts, limits, sources and the concurrency backstop are unchanged. | With no head role, no handover could be recorded. | — | The statement says "Commission handed to" and "Received by (employee) signature". |
